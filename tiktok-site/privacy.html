Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

This policy explains what information AutoClip (“the app”) handles when you connect your TikTok account and publish videos with it. The app runs on your own computer. The operator of this website does not run a server that receives your data.

1. Information the app handles

  • TikTok authorization data. When you connect your account, TikTok gives the app an access token, a refresh token, your TikTok open_id and your display name (scope user.info.basic).
  • Your videos. The source videos and the clips created from them are stored in a folder on your computer. When you choose to publish a clip, the app sends that video file and its caption to TikTok (scope video.publish).
  • Application settings and logs stored locally, for example the app key you entered and diagnostic messages.

2. How the information is used

Authorization data is used only to publish the videos you select to your TikTok account and to show which account is connected. Videos are processed locally to create clips. The data is not used for advertising, profiling or analytics.

3. Where the information is stored

Everything is stored locally on your computer in the application's data folder. Nothing is sent to the operator of this website or to any third party, except the videos and captions you choose to publish, which are sent to TikTok. TikTok processes them under its own Privacy Policy.

4. Sharing

The app does not sell, rent or share your information with anyone. The only recipient is TikTok, and only when you publish a video.

5. Retention and deletion

  • Use Disconnect in the app's TikTok settings to delete the stored tokens from your computer.
  • Revoke the app's access in TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security & permissions → Apps and services.
  • Delete the application's data folder to remove projects, clips and settings.

6. Security

Tokens are kept in a file in the application's data folder on your computer. Anyone with access to your computer account may be able to read it, so keep your computer secured.

7. Children

The app is not directed to children. Use of TikTok is subject to TikTok's own age requirements.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with an updated date.
